# Playing CPU, 2026-11-05

Five bit-identical savings in `AculyreEngine::process()`, found by
profiling the steel lyre harp demo with callgrind (instructions and its
cache simulation). Every commit renders the check set below byte for byte
as the 0.7.9 build does.

Build: the Rack SDK's code generation, clang -O3 `-fno-builtin
-fno-vectorize -fno-slp-vectorize -ffp-contract=off -fno-exceptions`,
C++17. Machine: a cloud container, Intel Xeon at 2.80 GHz, one thread.
Timing covers `Performer::process()` only (not `prepare()`), as a
percentage of one core at real time: the median of five runs of each
build, the builds alternated. Development-machine figures, never Reason or
DAW CPU.

Total: 38.95 % to 33.25 % of a core (-14.6 %). Oversampled, median of
three alternated runs: the steel demo at 96 kHz 29.2 % to 25.4 %, the
nylon demo at 192 kHz 59.9 % to 51.8 % (both -13 %). The machine moves a
few percent between runs, so a step under 3 % is within the noise; the
instruction counts are the deterministic figure.

Callgrind on the steel demo, `process()` only: 10.65 G to 9.83 G
instructions (-7.7 %), L1 read misses 599 M to 542 M (-9.5 %).

## What changed

- **Energy.** Each string's mean squared wave velocity was divided out
  every sample; only the control step and the observers read it.
- **String-bar coupling.** A string's drive on the bar's ten coordinates
  and the bar's motion at its end were scalar loops over the seven bends;
  they run as coordinate pairs and as string pairs, every sum in its old
  order.
- **Thiran sections.** The 56 fractional-delay sections live in
  `LoopFilters` for the block and run two at a time; the control step's
  retune hands a loop its section and takes it back.
- **Loop layout.** The fields every sample reads head each 64 KiB `Loop`,
  in its first 64 bytes, instead of spreading over several cache lines.
- **Dispersion pairs.** The bass strings' second-order sections run like
  the first-order stages: gathered by pair count, a pair at a time across
  the loops.

## It is the same sound

The check set, float output compared byte for byte against the 0.7.9
build: the three preset demos; the glissando on the steel harp with the
piezo at 0.7 and on the nylon harp at 96 kHz; the shared phrase on the gut
lyre at 192 kHz; the dynamics phrase without sympathy; the dry reference
A4; single notes at 44.1 kHz. Identical at every commit. `Aculyre.Engine`
and `Aculyre.DemoMidiSources` pass and `AculyreDSPCxx17` builds.

## Where the rest goes

At 4ec55ca the soundbox's modes take about 27 % of the instructions and
the strings' filter passes about 35 %; the bridge solve, the energy
tracking, the room and the output share the rest. About 18 % of reads
still miss L1: each sample walks string, mode and room state that by the
structures' sizes comes to some 45 KiB, more than the cache holds. The
modes' arithmetic is what the coupled model needs. The candidates left in
the profile, the control step's bend lookup and the energy tracking, are
each 1 to 3 % of the instructions.
