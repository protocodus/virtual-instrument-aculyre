# A reset that clears only what a read can reach, 2026-11-16 (0.13.13)

Against 0.13.12 (`543277c`). All timings are development-machine figures
from a 4-core Intel Xeon cloud container (Ubuntu clang 18.1.3), not Reason's
or a DAW's CPU.

## What was slow

`AculyreEngine::reset()` runs on the audio thread: the Rack adapter calls
`Performer::reset()` on Reason's transport reset, and the Performer resets
the engine it has faded out 20 ms after an instrument change. It cleared in
full every loop's line and pending ring (two 64 KB rings for each of 54
loops on a harp), every string's 64 KB mean-square ring and the room's
0.6 MB: about 9 MB of stores. `sleep()` cleared the squares and the room in
full too.

Engine reset, best of 20 after a ringing note (`std::chrono::steady_clock`):

| | Lyre Harp | Nylon Lyre Harp | Gut Lyre |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.13.12 | 390.2 µs | 389.7 µs | 236.7 µs |
| 0.13.13 | 15.0 µs | 14.6 µs | 7.3 µs |

Cold, the first reset after the rings were last touched took 1.2 to 1.55 ms.
A fresh Performer on the nylon harp (it starts by fading in from the steel
harp), four notes at block 2, 256-frame blocks: block 3, where the faded
engine resets, took 1195 µs (22.4% of the block) on 0.13.12 and 394 µs
(7.4%, a steady block's cost) on 0.13.13.

## The change

- `Loop::reset()` clears only `reach` samples behind the new write, where
  `reach` is the furthest any of the string's designs reads back (its
  longest line over the tabulated bends, or the energy window's round trip
  an octave down) plus the Thiran section's taps. `silence()` already relies
  on that bound, and it leaves `pending` all zero.
- The squares ring is cleared over the same reach behind `squaresIndex`;
  `noteOn` already rescales only that window.
- `Room::reset()` clears each ring only its own delay before position 0
  (the early taps' longest, each diffuser's and each FDN line's length).
- `setInstrument()` and `Room::prepare()`, which are not real time and may
  lengthen a reach, clear the whole rings before they reset.

## Parity

A 24-scenario Performer harness (48, 44.1 and 96 kHz; 1x, 2x and 4x
oversampling where the rate allows; all three presets; 64-, 97- and
256-frame blocks; switches mid-ring and within a fade, host resets while
strings ring, sleep and wake, bends, levers, mute, release and piezo under
held chords) writes every output sample. Its output is byte-identical to
0.13.12 in the Reason-like build (`-O2 -std=c++17 -fno-vectorize
-fno-slp-vectorize -fno-builtin -ffp-contract=off -fno-exceptions
-DACULYRE_DISABLE_AVX2`) and the native build (`-O3`). Leaving out the
line's clear in `Loop::reset()` changes all 24 scenarios, so the harness
sees a reset that clears too little.

`Aculyre.*` unit (14) and audio (5) tiers pass; `AculyreDSPCxx17` builds.

## Tried and not kept

- **The radiation sums of `stepPart` in locals.** Through the
  `Pair (&)[4]` references each group loads and stores all eight sums. In
  locals, x86's sixteen vector registers spill them instead: 1.2% more
  instructions for the steel harp's `dense` (274.66 to 277.99 M), none
  fewer for the gut lyre. It may pay on arm64's 32 registers; not measured.
- **A looser dispersion fit** (the stage search's 1-cent target at 2 and
  3 cents): 112 to 90 and 77 first-order stages on the steel harp, 126 to
  112 and 100 on the nylon. The dispersion sections are about 14% of a
  harp's instructions, so this saves under 3% while moving partials up to
  3.8 cents; not offered.
- **Stronger soundbox mode cuts**, offered as an A/B set instead (project
  files, `aculyre/2026-11-16-mode-cut-abc/`): a 70 dB floor keeps 188 of 289
  harp modes and saves 5.0% of a steel harp's median callback (2.1% gut); a
  60 dB floor keeps 145 and saves 11.3% (8.8% gut). Their nulls against
  0.13.13 are -51 to -65 dB and -27 to -48 dB under the signal.
  The 70 dB cut ships in 0.13.14 ([decisions](../decisions.md),
  2026-11-17): the 60 dB cut left the steel B2 6.25 cents flat at 2.5 s,
  past `Aculyre.Engine`'s 6.
